A Beginner's Guide to Badminton

What is Badminton?

Badminton is a racket sport played by two players in singles or four players in doubles. The goal is to hit a shuttlecock over a net and land it inside the opponent's court. This sport requires speed, coordination, and focus. It is a fun way to stay active, improve physical fitness, and develop teamwork and sportsmanship.
